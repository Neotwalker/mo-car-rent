# Stage 11 - Catalog UX v5

- Local Swiper 11.1.14 bundled in `assets/vendor/swiper/` (no CDN).
- `Популярные автомобили` rebuilt as a real Swiper with drag/swipe, navigation and draggable scrollbar.
- Catalog top rebuilt around rental search intent: pickup, return, date range, location refinement and a large filters modal.
- Date range picker is custom: 2 months on desktop, 1 on mobile, past dates disabled.
- Filters are modal-based and only use demo-supported vehicle data; unconfirmed business claims are intentionally excluded.
- Quick facet cards remain available for common catalog decisions.
- Filter count, active chips, sort and URL parameters are supported.
- Hover/focus feedback added for all major interactive controls; global fallback catches plain links/buttons.
